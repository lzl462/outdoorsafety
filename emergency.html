Emergency numbers

What it is Number
Emergency (police, fire, ambulance, search and rescue). 9-1-1
Poison Control 1-800-567-8911
BC Wildfire Service (report a wildfire) 1-800-663-5555
or *5555 from a cell
Marine / Howe Sound (Joint Rescue Coordination Centre). On the water, VHF Channel 16. 1-800-567-5111

Call 9-1-1. Tell the call taker where you are and what has happened. Stay on the line, answer their questions, and follow their instructions.

If you cannot connect, see No cell service. For immediate actions, see What to do if….
