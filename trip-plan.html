Trip Plan

Fill in your plan, review it, then give it to someone you trust. Agree when they should call 9-1-1 if you have not returned or checked in.

Blank printable plan

Before you start

Check your route and conditions, shared essentials and contact's role.

Fields marked * are required. Use the local date and time for your trip throughout, including the return and calling deadline.

The website does not save your entries. Leaving or reloading this page may lose them. Copy, email or print your reviewed plan to keep it.

Online filling needs JavaScript. You can still use the blank printable plan.

Please check the highlighted fields.

1. Your trip
Names of the people on the trip.
Trailhead, parking location or GPS coordinates.
Include the route out and back, and any planned alternative.
2. Contact and equipment
Numbers your trusted contact can pass to responders.
Device or contact details, if carried.
Optional. Include only what you want to share with your contact.
Use the existing essentials and activity lists. One item per line if useful.
3. Agree when your contact should act
Agree this deadline with the person who has your plan. It can be different from your expected return time.

Review and share

Check the complete plan, then send it yourself. Make sure your contact received it and knows when to act. This page cannot confirm delivery or monitor your return.

Leave this blank to choose the recipient in your email app.

If no email app opens, copy the plan into your email or a message instead. A long plan may need to be copied rather than opened as an email draft. Printed plans use a compact layout. Long notes may need extra pages.

Complete the required fields and review your plan before printing. You can also use the blank printable plan.
