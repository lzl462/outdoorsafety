Get help / No cell service

If your phone has no service

Reception can disappear soon after you leave the parking area. Plan another way to call for help.

On this page

If you need help nowBefore your trip

If you need help now

  1. Try calling 9-1-1. If you connect, explain your location and what has happened, answer questions, and follow instructions.
  2. If you cannot connect, use your satellite messenger or emergency beacon’s SOS function, if you have one, following its instructions.
  3. Keep the group together in a safe place. If the spot is unsafe, move only as needed to get out of immediate danger.
  4. Do not wander in search of reception. Stay warm, dry, and ready to signal for help.

If someone needs immediate first aid, use the hurt steps. Your trusted contact can call 9-1-1 and share your plan if you miss the return or check-in arrangement. See the contact’s job and waiting for help.

Before your trip

  • Download a map that works offline and learn how to find your GPS position.
  • Save phone battery for emergency communication and carry a separate light.
  • Choose an emergency communication device suited to the route and learn how to use its SOS emergency-alert function before leaving.
  • Leave a trip plan and an agreed return or check-in time with someone reliable.

An offline map helps with navigation. It does not send an SOS. A satellite messenger or emergency beacon has its own capabilities and limits; know yours.

Save the useful guides before you go. This website has no offline mode. Use your browser’s Print or Save as PDF option.
