Outdoor Safety · Sea-to-Sky DistrictCall 9-1-1 in an emergency

If you are lost

Print and keep in the pack

STOP: Stop. Think. Observe. Plan. Then call 9-1-1.
Back to site
  1. Stop walking. Stay together. Sit down if you need to calm the group.
  2. Call or text 9-1-1. Ask for police. Say you need Search and Rescue.
  3. Give GPS coordinates from the Compass or Maps app. Describe the last clear landmark.
  4. Stay put if you left a trip plan, unless the spot is immediately dangerous.
  5. Put on extra layers. Eat. Drink. Build or unpack shelter before dark.
  6. Signal: whistle in threes, bright clothing, fire or light at night if safe.
  7. Do not bushwhack downhill through cliffs. Many Sea-to-Sky slopes cliff out.

If there is no cell service

  • Try 9-1-1 anyway. Emergency calls can use any available tower.
  • Use a satellite messenger (inReach, Zoleo, SPOT) SOS.
  • Move only to a nearby ridge, clearing, or known trail if you are sure.
  • Keep one person with the injured or tired. Do not split the group far apart.

Educational handout. Not an official SAR or government document. Rescue in B.C. is free.
