Winter & avalanche checklist

This corridor is avalanche country, including some hiking trails.

No training + no kit = do not go into avalanche terrain.
Back to site

Check before you go

  • Read the Avalanche Canada bulletin: avalanche.ca
  • Mountain weather, not only the town forecast.
  • DriveBC for Highway 99. Stay off unmaintained forest service roads.
  • Resort boundary is not a safety boundary. Out-of-bounds is backcountry.

Every person in the group

  • Transceiver worn on the body, tested that morning.
  • Shovel and probe.
  • Partner who can search. Do not travel alone in avalanche terrain.
  • AST 1 (or equivalent) training at minimum.

If someone is buried

  1. Watch the last-seen point. Shout. Switch transceivers to search.
  2. Search, probe, shovel. Companions are the best chance of survival.
  3. Call 9-1-1 as soon as you can without stopping the search.
  4. Treat for trauma and hypothermia. Keep the airway clear.

Educational handout. Not an official SAR or government document. Rescue in B.C. is free.
