If someone is hurt

Call 9-1-1 early. Rescues in this corridor can take hours.

You will not be billed for Search and Rescue in British Columbia.
Back to site
  1. Make the scene safe. Do not become the second patient.
  2. Call 9-1-1. Ask for police. They activate SAR.
  3. Give: location (GPS), number of people, what happened, injuries, weather, and your phone number.
  4. Treat life-threatening bleeding. Keep the person warm and dry. Hypothermia is common here.
  5. Stay with them. Send a competent person to a cell-service spot only if 9-1-1 cannot connect.
  6. Prepare for a long wait: extra layers, headlamp, water, a way to signal a helicopter (bright tarp).

Tell the operator

  • Trail name and last junction (example: Rubble Creek, 2 km below Garibaldi Lake).
  • Whether you can walk, need a helicopter, or are on steep rock or snow.
  • If anyone has a satellite messenger.
