Prepare / Essentials

Pack for the wait, not only for the walk.

Light
a headlamp and spare batteries
Fire-making kit
matches or a lighter and a fire starter
Signalling
a whistle or signalling mirror
Extra food and water
enough for an unexpected delay
Extra clothing
warm layers and a rain shell
Navigation
map, compass, offline maps, a way to call for help
First aid kit
and know how to use it
Emergency shelter
an emergency blanket or shelter
Pocket knife
or multi-tool
Sun protection
sunglasses, sunscreen and a hat

The ten shared essentials, from AdventureSmart. Bring personal medication and spare glasses if you need them.
